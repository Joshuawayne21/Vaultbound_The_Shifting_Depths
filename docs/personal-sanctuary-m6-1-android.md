# Prismheart Sanctuary Stage 6.1 — device review route

Build **`0.7.0-home6a`**, Android version code **28**. Native checks passed; rendered review and physical Android testing remain pending.

Checklist for the functionally verified Stage 6.1 source build. No physical Android or rendered result is implied. Verify the published commit/version before importing; preserve the same application/user-data identity, back up the profile, and do not uninstall or clear app data. A source ZIP is not an APK.

For early access to implemented home/depth gates, first enable **SETTINGS → TEST ACCESS → ON → REVIEW CHANGE → CONFIRM & SAVE** once on the backed-up device profile. Check **TEST ACCESS ON** after restart. Ownership, costs and secured Spiritweaving ingredients/memory provenance still apply; no achievement is granted. This setting does not unlock unfinished city services or change the DEMO market into real trading.

1. Record device, Android/Godot versions, resolution, settings, profile baseline and actual frame-time measurement method. Begin at Medium / 30 FPS target; a target is not a measurement.
2. Walk from the normal arrival around the six central counters and the Prismheart. Confirm six clear inward-facing prompts, readable **DEMO / NOT LIVE** labels, walkable gaps and no collision trap. The legacy City Guide must still work.
3. Open one booth with the touch interaction, place an eligible TEST order and inspect the same order/balance through another booth. Close/change booths during review and retry a stale confirmation. Real Depthmarks, inventory and progression must remain unchanged by demo actions.
4. Recruit Guardian/Mystic, choose an owned cosmetic pet and walk both inner and outer circuits, all eight districts and both terrace ramps. Repeat from dungeon, Abyss and Hall return points. Watch for blocked paths, oscillation, teleport recovery and missing followers.
5. Switch third-person/isometric cameras during movement beneath and around the Prismheart, at every booth, near walls/towers and at portal fronts. Inspect minimum/maximum pitch and multiple orbit headings. No visible opaque surface should allow a through-wall view; retraction must remain readable.
6. Check the tall ivory walls, blue/gold reliefs, varied exterior towers and raised tree/cascade silhouettes by day and night. Record screenshots; headless bounds do not prove attractive composition. Exterior towers and distant scenery are decorative, without promised walkable overlooks.
7. Test Low/Medium/High, the optional 30/60 FPS targets, reduced motion/effects and zero ambient density. The targets do not certify device performance. Essential counter/wall/tower silhouettes and collision must remain. Crystal motion should freeze under reduced motion; optional particles reduce/stop without hiding interactions.
8. Enter an eligible dungeon and return; enter the unchanged southeast Personal Sanctuary gate and visit all six new home rooms, then return. Ground-floor routes are Armory, Companion Hall and Relic Library; upper-gallery routes are Personal Chamber, Wardrobe and Rooftop Garden. Use each room's return to the foyer, then return to the city. Confirm companions, both cameras, loot, merchant/forge transactions, equipment, pets, home identity and saves still behave correctly.
9. Measure sustained frame times, memory, shadow/overdraw cost and thermal behavior with scene/settings/duration recorded. Inspect notches, UI scale, rapid taps, drag-off and app background/resume. Report pass/fail/not tested and attach screenshots/video where possible.

The city's cardinal-wing rearrangement, Bank/Guild/Undercroft, new contracts, live market and real purchasing are outside Stage 6.1. Their absence is not a reason to invent online activity or call a demo transaction real trading.
