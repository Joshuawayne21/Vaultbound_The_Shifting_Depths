## Implemented scope

Beastwilds is the first local discovery expedition behind its existing Nexus gateway. Four bounded habitats contain three required claims (`trail_marker`, `glade_egg`, `pool_rescue`) and optional `forage_cache`. A registered southern Return spring offers reviewed **SECURE & RETURN** after all required finds, or disclosed **ABANDON & RETURN** while incomplete. Both route back to the Nexus. The same existing player, controller, both cameras, Guardian/Mystic, selected cosmetic pet, inventory, bag and Session are reused.

Normal eligibility requires an **earned Depth 2 clear and extraction**; merely unlocking Depth 2 is insufficient. The existing offline TEST ACCESS switch can bypass the earned gate without granting progression or findings. The activity uses a fixed authored circular path with seeded discovery offsets/scenery, not a full new procedural combat biome.

| Crystalwater Refuge | Required spirit rescue | Existing Spiritweaving ingredients/memory and configured 6–10 DM |

Findings remain unsecured until the single completion save. There is no direct pet capture or ownership grant, new species/genetics, combat/boss encounter, activity XP, trophy, Realm Mastery, Realm Seal or mask gameplay. The user explicitly deferred Masks of the Abyss; existing mask collectibles remain unchanged. Decorative wildlife uses existing native presentation families and is not owned. Seven other major activities remain unavailable: Echoes, Gilded Vault, Titan's Gate, Shattered Rift, Guild Conquest, Chaos Gate and Forbidden Gate.

The Nexus still has ten major gateway identities; exactly three route to implemented gameplay: Abyssal's existing progressive combat expedition, Bloodspire's existing NPC practice and Beastwilds discovery. The auxiliary Sunken Vault practice route, western Personal Sanctuary, Sanctuary services, six shared DEMO Exchange booths and all previous worlds remain. Beastwilds is the sole new fifteenth world. No live marketplace, authenticated server, multiplayer, ranking, guild, payment or hosting service is introduced.

## Save and reward preservation

Profile **v12** adds **activity namespace v1** while retaining home namespace **v3**. A monotonic visit serial, one open interruption ticket and typed Beastwilds records track entry/completion/death/abandonment, secured egg/rescue counts, earned DM and completion timing. They do not impersonate dungeon records. Supported older data loads without rewriting files; the first valid migrated write preserves exact original bytes in `.pre-v12.bak` through the existing verified temporary-write/backup/atomic-rename process. Earlier backups remain. Unknown future namespaces, records and ticket fields are protected instead of discarded.

A new visit reserves one entry revision and space for its final settlement. At the last available revision, optional persisted equipment/supply changes cannot consume that reservation; secure/abandon remains available. A failed initial reservation writes nothing. A later rejected admission may legitimately record the reserved/abandoned attempt while leaving secured possessions unchanged. Replacement generations must not be erased by cleanup from an older callback.

The source-verified successful completion updates actual DM/materials/Spiritweaving ingredients and separate activity records. It does not advance dungeon clears/unlocks, XP, companion XP, Rootwarden counters, merchant rotation or existing pet-secret achievements. The existing lifetime `statistics.total_gold` increases by exactly the secured DM; this is expected earned-currency accounting, not a dungeon-clear grant. Existing pet ownership and achievements remain intact.

Save failure keeps the live source and bag retryable. After a successful save, a failed Nexus arrival retains the closed world and its original completed exit proof; retry performs travel without a second payout. Incomplete abandonment and actual death/party wipe lose only unsecured findings. A healthy below-world recovery preserves the visit, seed, serial, claims, health and revive allowance. Quitting/process eviction does not resume unsecured exploration; the persistent ticket is recovery/replay bookkeeping, retired without rewards on a later admission.

Diagnostic runs and their counts are entirely excluded from the release total.
