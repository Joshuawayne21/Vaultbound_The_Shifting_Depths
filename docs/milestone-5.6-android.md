# Milestone 5.6 Android regression review

This is a manual acceptance checklist, not a record of completed device tests. Use the [README import instructions and controls](../README.md#import-and-play-on-android), the [current automated commands](../README.md#validation) and [checkpoint validation](milestone-5.6-validation.md). Preserve the earlier regression coverage in [the test plan](test-plan.md); its archived counts and UI names describe earlier builds.

**Version 0.5.6 has passed its automated release gate; physical-device acceptance is pending.** The integrated catalog contains **350 noncurrency definitions: 300 available and 50 unavailable future entries**, plus the preserved currency definition (**351 total**). Item inspection, animated Hall displays and Rare+ notifications are integrated. The [catalog record](catalog-batches.md) and [final validation report](milestone-5.6-validation.md) document the delivered scope and 10,519 passing assertions. A passing headless gate does not complete this physical-device review.

## Prepare and record

1. Import the full repository's root `project.godot` into the **Godot 4.6.3 Android editor**, standard GDScript edition, using **Compatibility**. Wait for import, run the project/main scene and check the Android debugger for missing resources or script/shader errors. Record the exact tested commit. Running in the editor needs no APK; export/signing is a separate check.
2. Use a copy of each development save. Back up `user://vaultbound/profile.json`, its `.bak` and existing migration snapshots before changing versions; keep an independent copy outside the test profile. Android app-specific paths differ between the editor and an exported app. Do not delete or overwrite the only original save to pass a test.
3. Record device/model, Android and Godot versions, renderer, resolution/aspect ratio, cutout/navigation mode, graphics tier, FPS target, UI scale, reduced-motion/effects settings, party size and displayed dungeon seed. Use narrow and wide landscape devices where available; rotate between the two landscape orientations and exercise Android system gestures. Portrait gameplay is outside scope. Start with **Medium / 30 FPS**.

## Saves, equipment and touch

1. Load backed-up version-one, version-two and version-three profiles separately, plus a current version-four profile. Compare Depthmarks, quantities, unique equipment IDs, rolls, enhancements, protection flags, equipment, companions, talents, discoveries, displays and expedition history against the originals. Loading a legacy profile must not rewrite it. Before the first successful migrated write, confirm the exact original bytes are retained in `.pre-v4.bak` (or the content-addressed snapshot for a separately restored legacy record); earlier `.pre-v3` snapshots must remain. Restart after a saved change and compare again. If device storage tools cannot inspect the bytes, record that part as unverified.
2. Check legacy reserve entries when old holdings exceed forty active inventory slots. Claim supported quantities as space becomes available; unknown IDs must remain preserved. On disposable copies only, verify corrupt-primary recovery from a valid `.bak`, and that newer-format or structurally valid unavailable-owned-content profiles show a protected/read-only warning instead of silently replacing progress. Keep original and recovered files for any failure report.
3. Open **Inventory** and **Equipment** separately. Browse all eleven slots: **Main Weapon, Secondary Weapon, Shield, Helmet, Chest Armor, Gloves, Leg Armor, Boots, Necklace, Ring and Charm**. Assign owned sword and staff instances to the two loadouts. Check the correct icon, active highlight, model, ability labels and comparison; empty loadouts must be disabled without a fictional weapon. Restart with Secondary active and verify the same assignments. While wounded, repeated gear changes must not heal or stack inactive-weapon bonuses.
4. In both cameras, move while tapping loadouts and swiping the camera. Try switching during attacks, charged heavy, defense and dodge: incompatible switches must be rejected without changing that action. Accepted switches use the brief 0.32-second lock. Start an ability, switch away/back and verify its cooldown continues. Switch cameras during melee and a staff projectile; one attack must not gain an extra hit, effect roll or projectile. Changing equipment in a paused menu must allow the short switch lock to finish without advancing combat cooldowns.
5. Use at least three simultaneous fingers for joystick, camera and attack/loadout controls. Release in different orders, drag off targets, cancel a touch, and put two fingers on a held control; only its final owner release should end that hold. Open each menu while moving, charging or guarding, then background/resume the app. The world and companions must pause in menus; closing/resuming must clear stale movement, defense and charged attacks. Repeat during travel, purchase confirmation, inventory scroll and preview drag.
6. Try **100%, 110% and 120% UI scale** at every supported landscape aspect ratio. Check cutout/system-bar safe areas, readable tooltips and rarity names/I–VII markers, larger equipment pages, the forty-slot inventory and independent stat/lore scrolling. Exercise filters, sorting, compare, lock and favorite without scroll gestures selecting or buying. Rotate the character preview and item inspector with one finger while another touches elsewhere; neither gesture may move the world camera. Back must restore the prior selection and keep the menu paused. Close/reopen both previews repeatedly and inspect frame pacing while hidden. Save settings, restart and verify them independently of progression.

## Loot, commerce and extraction

1. Enter Rootbound Abyss, record the seed and collect room treasure plus normal, elite and Rootwarden searchable caches. Check safe reachable placement and **USE → Search → Take / Take All**, quantities, remaining rows and disappearing empty caches. Repeat taps, cancel/scroll, leave/re-enter rooms and search from behind a wall: none may duplicate or remotely claim a reward. Near full capacity, a rejected take must retain the reward; a partial take must retain its remainder. An over-capacity room chest must remain unclaimed.
2. Compare displayed **Depthmarks (DM)** and material quantities before/after collection and each quote; old saved currency amounts must remain unchanged by the rename. Loot stays **unsecured** until extraction. Rare through Ancient Relic accepted rewards should show the noninteractive **UNSECURED · Extract to keep** card when HUD space allows. Inspection, rejected transfers and duplicate touches must not announce new rewards. Open menus while a card is waiting and verify it does not obscure controls or carry over into another run.
3. Buy a small stack, cancel a second quote, sell/salvage an expendable spare, repair and craft at the existing Sanctuary services. Try two-finger and repeated confirmation, insufficient DM/materials, stale quotes and full inventory. Only one accepted transaction may change ownership, balance or stock. Locked, favorite, equipped and displayed items must resist sale/salvage; protected materials must not be consumed. Appraisal must reveal the same owned instance after the quoted fee. Merchant rotations follow completed expeditions, not device-clock changes.
4. At the Blacksmith, review exact DM/material costs and current → next statistics before confirming. Check **+5 caps for Common through Legendary** and **+10 caps for Mythic/Ancient Relic**, including an eligible accessory. Test an unaffordable preview, capped item, broken/unidentified item and protected material stack. Follow [the enhancement material chain](enhancement-policy.md), including earned elite fragments, Rootwarden Essence and crafted cores/heartstones; no unfinished encounter is required. A guaranteed upgrade changes the same UID once, retains its rolls/protections/display assignment and persists after restart. Compare success feedback at all UI scales with reduced motion/effects on and off.
5. Read effect percentages and compare real monster combat with appropriate gear: critical chance, lifesteal, block strength and ability cooldown reduction. Use [the effect caps](equipment-effects.md) as the reference: 20% critical chance, 4% lifesteal/at most 4 HP per entire attack, +20% block strength and 15% ability recovery. Attack a training target, hit cover and repeat a camera/loadout change after a staff cast: rejected hits must not heal and one attack must not reset its effect budget. Upgrades must not multiply rolled or proc percentages. No elemental-resistance behavior is expected.
6. Defeat the Rootwarden, collect its grand cache and extract at the connected spring. Check the single boss reward/Heart, results, secured DM/items, character and companion XP, discoveries and statistics. Restart and inspect equipment, upgrades and Hall assignments. Then collect new loot and separately test death/party wipe, retreat and closing the app mid-run. Only the new unsecured bag is lost; secured possessions/progression survive. Mid-expedition resume is not implemented.

## Collection, presentation and preserved play

1. Visit the **Collection Hall** with companions in both cameras. Assign/clear occupied equipment stands and relic pedestals, inspect the selected item, leave/re-enter and restart. The displayed reference must retain its owned UID without consuming, duplicating or moving inventory; the same instance cannot occupy multiple stands. Check the Rootwarden trophy and journals. Completion counts only secured discoveries of obtainable noncurrency definitions; unavailable future records must not lower it.
2. Inspect equipment, stackable valuables and trophy entries from Inventory, Hall and read-only catalog views. Check rarity label/runes, enhancement, durability, lore, quantity and ownership origin. Stock, catalog, unsecured stacks and legacy overflow must not gain invented UIDs. Rotate the item, scroll lore, cancel/Back, suspend/resume, and reopen after a saved upgrade. The viewer must show the updated item without creating ownership. Shared family models and missing art must be labeled honestly.
3. Review display motion, rarity accents, enhancement cues and depth occlusion on **Low/Medium/High**, with reduced motion/effects enabled and disabled. Reduced motion should stop decorative movement; reduced effects should remove sparks while preserving readable rarity and essential attack warnings. Check hand grips, floor contact, armor/cape clipping, sword/staff visibility and idle/walk/run/block/cast/dodge/hit/downed poses on player, Guardian, Mystic and previews. This is original primitive-mesh/procedural prototype art with shared family icons/models; no production rig, skinned armor, authored animation clips or bespoke item/trophy art is claimed.
4. Read the future catalog entries without trying to unlock them: greatswords, dual blades, spears, bows, scythes and magical gauntlets have no working combat family; elemental-resistance potions have no implemented damage/resistance system; future-boss trophies have no corresponding encounter. These entries must remain unavailable, absent from loot/stock/recipes and rejected for ownership/use/enhancement. Supported combat remains sword/shield and staff; the existing Rootwarden is the expedition boss.
5. Retain the original Sanctuary → practice dungeon → arena round trip, the Hall passage and full Abyss expedition. Rapidly repeat portal taps during fades and verify one destination, preserved party/resources, no stale effects/targets or held input. Play at least three seeds, including optional branches, secret passages and backtracking. Check minimap discovery, camera obstruction, cover, room collision, hazards and Rootwarden warnings/Fury in both cameras, including switches during attacks and revive channels.
6. Repeat solo, with one companion and with both Rowan and Lyra. Recruit/dismiss/restart; test Follow, Attack My Target, Hold, Retreat and Revive. Observe doorway navigation, Guardian blocking, Mystic healing, interrupted revives, finite rescue limits, bleedout and one party-wipe outcome. Extract with an ally downed/separated and confirm the saved roster returns. Companions must retain ordinary health/stamina, cover and cooldown rules and never consume the player's collected treasure.

## Sustained performance record

Use the same seed, route, party and viewpoints when comparing **Low, Medium and High** at **30 and 60 FPS targets**. Warm up first; sample at least 15 minutes per tested configuration across Sanctuary services, occupied Hall, menus/previews, 8–12-room expeditions, maximum normal encounters, boss combat, extraction and repeated travel/suspend/resume. Prioritize a stable 30 FPS experience before assessing 60 FPS. Record observed FPS, frame-time spikes, draw calls/active enemies, memory where tooling permits, heat/throttling, battery change, touch latency and audio. A selected target or brief idle sample is not a measured guarantee.
