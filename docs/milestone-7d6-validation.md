# Milestone 7D.6 — Crimson Ember Temple validation

The accepted frozen source passed **43,302 assertions across 186 suites and 221 runtime invocations**, including 22 independent restart readers. All 196 accepted 7D.5 modes are retained, alongside 25 new modes from 19 suites. Import, all 89 strict script loads and 120 startup frames passed. No runtime warning, script error, native leak or timeout was accepted.

Only this approved Ember batch is complete; later Milestone 7 work remains outside this report.

## Implemented scope

The public Content 6 plan preserves Rootbound-only depths 1–5 and the prior Rootbound/Crystal selection at depths 6–10. At depth 11 and above, an independent Ember choice precedes the existing Rootbound/Crystal selection. Theme-specific room, ordinary, boss, optional, hazard and reward choices are frozen at issuance. Profile 7, generator/depth-policy contracts and secured old theme history remain unchanged.

Fourteen Ember room roles use original native prototype props and materials. Pinned source hashes preserve original Rootbound/Crystal authored assets. Native tests inspect their retained room contracts and the fourteen new Ember modules, including floors, walls, connectors, populated chests, Return approaches and foreign-world exclusion. Raw surface arrays and actual indices establish native triangle positions. Raw family labels `sentinel`, `conductor` and `brood_keeper` map to Furnace Warden, Cinder Liturgist and Ash Sealkeeper. The inherited raw `EMBER LANCEKEEPER` metric label records the actual Cinder Pikewarden pose section; source identity and gameplay assertions use Pikewarden. Instanced dressing uses opt-in captured submitted transforms under the dummy renderer and cross-checks native buffer readback when available. These checks do not establish rendered art quality, GPU clipping or device performance.

Furnace Warden has a grounded charge whose private travel evidence is available only after legitimate native movement. Backdraft consumes that evidence once. The stronger schedule uses separately warned opposed sectors with a harmless gap and one full final recovery. Cinder Liturgist uses independently warned finite stages. Ash Sealkeeper admits bounded native skeleton/archer support with original member identities, whole warning clocks and exact source ownership. Cinder Pikewarden uses bounded native motion near its home area, including actual wall-slide and displaced-return probes. Historical controllers and schema versions retain their explicit coverage.

Dormant mines are world-owned source opportunities. A dormant marker grants no damage authority. Genuine eligible party proximity, height, LOS and native site checks precede the full warning duration. Refused admission has bounded retries; shown/cancelled attempts do not silently rearm. Initial and Awareness hazards have distinct accepted serials and reservation clocks. Pending stages and in-flight dispatch remain relevant to safe Return, while an unarmed marker alone is not active damage.

Seven Ember reward tables use existing catalog items. Independent source-table enumeration and whole-file comparison allow only the 20 approved source-tag additions; the catalog remains 350 noncurrency items plus the existing currency row. Actual registered deaths produce private receipts and searchable caches with issued scaling once. Support actors do not grant independent loot, currency, XP, discoveries or objective completion. Local anti-replay receipt bookkeeping for a none pet profile does not grant pet payloads. Failed saves, partial collection, early Return, full completion, death and separate-process secured persistence are distinct scenarios.

The existing portal and HUD disclose the eligible pools and actual issued names. Native font checks preserve complete text and measure title, R/E legend, graph, room glyphs and footer at supported canvas/scale/inset combinations. Companion checks use actual registered actors, delayed decisions, existing commands, ordinary dodges, native routes and both cameras. Query budgets and danger-clearance thresholds remain unchanged.

The corrected fixture checks rejection just before the real edge, then advances the actual remaining duration; production timing is unchanged.

The correction preserves this safe refusal and separately performs real partial movement before inserting a raised native obstruction, to test collision and recovery after actual travel; its corrected retry passed. The mine fixture disabled controls on its only nearby companion. The producer correctly found no eligible party target and remained dormant. The correction retains the disabled negative and uses a real eligible companion while briefly holding movement until the actual warning is accepted.

Native minimap diagnostics measured a 2-pixel vertical title/legend overlap at 120 percent for the full one-line Ember title. Width, graph, glyph and footer checks remained valid. The production legend baseline now respects actual font metrics without changing the font, full title or panel. The same native helper also checks actual Rootbound R and E presentations.

The first integrated corrective retry reached the real raised-wall collision and exposed a production eligibility error: the shared party-target helper attempted to convert absent wall health to a number. The shared eligibility helper now validates finite numeric health and Boolean control state before conversion, preserving valid callers without requiring a group. ## Limits

No screenshot/video, APK export, live backend, hosting, payment or ranked service is tested. Host timings are scoped to this machine. Local profile/source guards are not authenticated online authority. Dungeon runs do not resume after restart; secured progress persists. Ancient 7E and later content remain outside this batch.
