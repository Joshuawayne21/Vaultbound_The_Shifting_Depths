The accepted Stages 2–4 foundation adds a separate north Nexus interior, ten individually styled major gateway sources in three galleries, one secondary Realm Conduit, orientation/map waypoints, common catalog-backed information/admission, and existing practice-dungeon/city-return auxiliary portals. The hall uses original native-mesh prototype architecture, ornaments and effects. Rendered composition and premium asset quality still require review; no finished sculpted characters or new portal sound library is claimed.

The city has **two actual portals**: north Nexus and west Personal Sanctuary. The north entrance replaces the former direct Rootbound route while retaining its map identity. Two former practice-portal locations become honest route/preparation guides; their playable destinations move behind the Nexus. The training sentinel, six shared Exchange booths, one Living Prismheart, existing fourteen city POIs, eight bridges and western home destination remain.

## Guarded integration and diagnostics

The sole protected-file exception is `scripts/systems/game.gd`. Its exact before/after hashes and authorization are pinned in the machine report: registering the hall, source-bound cards/deferred travel, actual-floor arrival rollback and Nexus-origin practice return. Existing progression, combat, party, home, pet, item, loot and demo-custody authorities retain their protected pins. This is local validation, not a server-authoritative implementation.

A four-suite development diagnostic passed its assertions but was correctly rejected for engine errors from a test lambda retaining a freed old arena Node. The test now captures an immutable instance ID while continuing to require actual REST & RESET replacement and the correct return. The final gate reran all four suites cleanly. An earlier registry fixture omitted the seven existing home rooms; it was corrected to assert the full fourteen-world registry rather than weakening preservation.

The unedited visual integration suite performs four fewer per-source checks because four city portals intentionally become two; twelve actual hall portal sources and relocated gameplay routes receive the new geometry, authority and travel checks. Neither the rejected diagnostic nor the separate native budget's fourteen checks are included in 11,226.
