# Sanctuary courtyard polish — Android review route

Build **`0.7.0-home6b`**, Android export version code **29**, passed its scoped native gate (8,435 checks / 33 modes). No rendered or physical Android result is implied by this checklist.

1. Back up the existing phone profile and its backups. Download the current published **main → Code → Download ZIP** from [GitHub](https://github.com/Joshuawayne21/Vaultbound_The_Shifting_Depths), or use the [main source ZIP](https://github.com/Joshuawayne21/Vaultbound_The_Shifting_Depths/archive/refs/heads/main.zip). Import a fresh extraction's root `project.godot` with Godot 4.6.3 Compatibility, verify `0.7.0-home6b`, and use **Run Project**. Preserve the same application/user-data identity; do not uninstall or clear app data. A source ZIP does not install an APK or migrate profiles between applications.
2. Record device, Android/Godot versions, resolution, build/commit, camera mode and settings. Record baseline inventory, Depthmarks, equipment, recruits, pets, home displays and unlocked depths. If needed, enable **SETTINGS → TEST ACCESS → ON → REVIEW CHANGE → CONFIRM & SAVE** once, then verify the saved badge after restart. Ownership and material costs still apply.
3. Begin at Medium / 30 FPS target. Walk the Exchange's inner circulation, six booth approaches, City Guide and outer ring. Confirm the enclosure feels spacious, signs remain readable, walls are visibly smaller, and all eight arch entrances are easy to identify. Every booth must still say **DEMO / NOT LIVE** and use the same TEST market; demo actions must not change real currency or items.
4. Recruit Guardian and Mystic. Walk outward and back through every arch and bridge: **A Portal Promenade, B Proving Grounds, C Merchant Terraces, D Personal Sanctuary, E Forge, F Appearance Preview, G Conservatory, H Tavern**. Walk near both sides of each span, stop on slopes, reverse direction and issue Follow/Hold/Retreat where appropriate. Check continuous support, clean deck/ramp seams, no invisible wall, no stuck follower and no visible recovery teleport. Forge/Tavern must retain their original ascent.
5. Repeat the route with an owned ground pet and a flying pet. Pets must follow without blocking movement, trading, targeting or portal use. This manual combination must not be inferred from a native route that only observes the player and two AI companions.
6. Switch third-person/isometric cameras while approaching, passing beneath and leaving every arch; inspect bridge rails, low walls and the Prismheart. Orbit and change third-person pitch near both sides of the perimeter. Retraction must preserve useful visibility and opaque surfaces must not allow a through-wall view.
7. Reproduce the reported wall flicker: slowly strafe and turn alongside the ivory wall, gold coping/bands, blue panels and runes, at close and medium distance. Repeat the same route and view in Low, Medium and High, by day and night. Record a short video, settings and location if flicker remains. Low disables the existing shadow key; a difference confined to shadow-enabled presets is a useful diagnostic, not proof that geometry is correct or a reason to ignore the issue.
8. Inspect the new relief and cap edges for stable color, buried banners, floating trim, flashing seams and gaps. Compare saved captures from home6a only on a disposable copied profile; do not load an older application over the only copy of current progress. The native repair removes confirmed coplanar overlaps, but visual confirmation still requires a rendered device run.
9. Inspect water beside/under each bridge, shoreline transitions, flower colors and distant canopies from both cameras. Water should read as shallow ornament without covering walkable decks or introducing damage. Flowers must not obscure service prompts or enter the road/terrace clearance. All material variants should remain consistent with the city.
10. Test Low/Medium/High, 0/50/100% ambient density, reduced motion/effects, supported UI scales and optional 30/60 FPS targets. Essential walls, arch shapes, bridge collision and prompts must remain. Optional effects may reduce; lower quality must not remove a visibility blocker. Targets and headless counts are not measured frame rates.
11. Visit the merchants/Blacksmith, recruit/dismiss through the existing Tavern, and enter/return from the practice dungeon, an eligible expedition and Personal Sanctuary. In the home, visit the foyer, Trophy Hall, Conservatory and the six added rooms; return to the city. Verify saved gear, pets, trophies, recruits, home identity and the TEST ACCESS choice after restart. Existing combat, weapon/camera switching, loot and extraction must still work.
12. Measure sustained frame times, memory, battery/thermal behavior and touch responsiveness with scene, quality, resolution and duration recorded. Check phone notches/system bars, drag-off, rapid taps and background/resume. Report each case as **pass / fail / not tested**, with screenshots/video where available.

This pass does not supply online guilds, a Bank, real player trades, ranked PvP or payments. It does not claim the full cardinal-district blueprint or a new sky system is complete. Keep real saves intact when reporting a failure.
