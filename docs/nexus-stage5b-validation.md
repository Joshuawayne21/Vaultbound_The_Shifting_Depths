## Remaining development and manual review

Echoes is a finite local trial. Endless survival, new bosses, trophies, mastery, masks and online co-op are outside this checkpoint. Masks are user-deferred. Gilded Vault, Titan's Gate, Shattered Rift, Guild Conquest, Chaos Gate and Forbidden Gate remain unavailable; isolated Gilded drafts are not implemented in this release. Competitive services still need authenticated authoritative infrastructure; no hosting deployment or billing is included.

Rendered composition, combat readability, camera occlusion in motion, Android touch comfort/notches, largest text scale, lifecycle interruptions, thermal performance and real combat difficulty remain manual checks in [the Android route](nexus-stage5b-android.md). Verify the source version after downloading GitHub; this report does not install or update a phone.
