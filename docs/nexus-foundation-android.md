# Nexus foundation — Android review checklist

For **`0.7.0-nexus2` / Android code 31**, which passed 11,226 native checks across 49 modes and 47 suites. Use the published version and compare root `project.godot` before launching. No physical Android testing has been performed. Keep the original phone profile and backups; do not clear application data to test an update. Record exact build/commit, device, OS, Godot renderer, resolution, quality, UI scale and pass/fail/not-tested results.

1. Start from the southern public city foyer. Check joystick, both cameras, Guardian/Mystic and the active cosmetic pet. Visit retained east merchants/forge, west home/pets/appearance, south recruitment and all six shared DEMO Exchange booths. Confirm saved DM, equipment, pets, trophies and home identity match the pre-update record.
2. Walk to northern district A. Open the **NEXUS OF REALMS** card and cancel once; then confirm entry. Confirm loading presents a supported floor, intact party and a reachable south return portal. The city Practice Sentinel and nearby guide boards must remain accessible. No board should claim contracts or matchmaking exist.
3. Walk the hall perimeter, inner circulation and every portal approach. Guardian/Mystic should navigate around the Conduit and furniture without oscillation, obstruction or recovery jumps. Switch cameras while moving, inspect upper ornaments and the central receiver, and check no opaque architecture clips through the camera.
4. Inspect all ten gateway cards. Check unique names, Adventure/Conflict/Ascension groups, visibly distinct ornaments/materials, actual mode, requirements and reward descriptions. Eight unfinished activities must say unavailable with no Enter route, including when TEST ACCESS is enabled. Proposed prerequisites must not masquerade as earned progress.
5. Use the Nexus orientation guide and map. Waypoints should point to the correct gallery or gate, preserve the player position and require approaching the real source before interaction. The guide must not open a synthetic Exchange market inside the hall.
6. At Abyssal Descent, review the existing depth selector. Check locked depth reasons, eligible theme preview, current party and confirmation. Cancel and reopen; enter an eligible floor, complete an expedition and extract. Verify actual rewards/depth unlocks persist after restart. On a disposable failure run, secured gear/home/pets must remain unchanged. Early returns retain the existing incomplete-depth behavior.
7. At Bloodspire, confirm **LOCAL PRACTICE** and existing NPC encounters. Check attack/dodge/weapon abilities, both cameras and companions. No ranked rating, PvP rewards, matchmaking or fake human opponents should appear. Return to the Nexus and confirm a safe arrival. Check existing death/respawn/return behavior with a disposable profile.
8. Enter the auxiliary Sunken Vault practice gate and return to Sanctuary. Re-enter the Nexus and use its south Sanctuary gate. Repeated ENTER taps, opening/canceling, moving out of reach before entry, background/resume and a failed load must not duplicate travel, rewards or party members.
9. From Sanctuary, visit the unchanged western Personal Sanctuary. Verify all unlocked home rooms, equipment/pet displays, trophy records, profile theme and identity, then return. The Nexus adds no second home or collection economy.
10. Review Low/Medium/High, 0/50/100% decorative density, reduced effects/motion and supported UI scales. Opaque blockers and walkable routes must remain consistent. Check portal surfaces/trim for flicker, legible labels at both camera distances, tap targets and phone cutouts. Decorative effects must not hide required controls.
11. Record sustained frame times, memory, temperature and loading pauses in the city, hall with all gateways visible, local practice and a representative expedition. A configured 30/60 FPS limit or a headless check is not proof of sustained phone performance. Record screenshots/video for any overlap, missing material, clipping, inaccessible portal or misleading availability.

Do not use these steps as acceptance evidence until actual results are recorded. No real online activity, payment, guild system, mask gameplay, mastery/seal rewards or physical-device performance is claimed by the foundation.
