## Mobile limits and risks

The added booth/tower architecture measures 4,722 triangles across 226 unbatched nonempty mesh objects, within its 24,000-triangle source budget. This is only the new layer: the separate native budget capture measures 102,629 allocated instance triangles for the whole active Sanctuary. See the report for visibility/node limits. Prismheart uses one lightweight pose update at 10 Hz Low / 20 Hz other presets and at most twelve instanced motes. Reduced motion freezes its pose; reduced effects caps motes at four; zero ambient density removes them. Opaque geometry and collision remain present across presets. No extra real-time lights or full-screen effects were added.

Native checks verify tower/crystal camera envelopes contain the visible geometry through tested animation poses. Taller walls can increase screen coverage, camera retraction and shadow cost even when triangle count is modest. Rendered camera framing and physical Android frame times/thermals are required before claiming visual or performance acceptance. Native geometry budgets and clearance checks do not establish 30 FPS.
